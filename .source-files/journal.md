## LORE AND DISCOVERIES
- **Morgalath (Aerthion's pages):**
  - **Nature:** about 1,400 years old; the Weaver's hound, who means to be "first among its threads."
  - **Sight:** **blind on the left**; a drake always flies at his left wing, and if it falls he turns to keep the wind on his left.
  - **The ember:** a charm gaze. He must see the face, within a hundred paces. Never refused.
  - **The roar:** breaks spears.
  - **Magic:** casts as a mage. Swallows spells, breaks wards, takes a man's shape. Travels only on wings.
  - **Resistance to binding:** shrugged off four bindings on the mountain; fewer away from Kharûn.
  - **Fire:** breathes from above into the thickest knot of foes, then must wait between breaths. Won't breathe on Ais.
  - **Fears:** cold (won't set foot on the Whispering Ice) and water.
  - **Habits:** talks always; calls everyone "little"; counts his gold at dawn; flies his bounds at dusk.
  - **The song:** **left the hall when the Song of the White Tree was hummed.**
- **Aldren's death:** told above. He called Ais's name, not a scream.
- **The silences:** locks, not shields. Woven of oaths and faith kept. A living song quickens them; a bell's echo doesn't.
- **The young tree:** can't be moved and isn't a creature. Grows with song and healthy land. Turns the hidden ways. Feels the other silences along the song.
- **The Bough-House and the Bough-Giving:** the door opens to the Tree's own wood. The walls hold a thousand years of songs, including the Song of the Bough's Becoming (four voices or more, sunset to sunrise).
- **Teleport keys:** the Ironbrow writ (Haldrik's hall); the silver leaf (the heart of Uchkeken, six months).
- **Rules of the fight** (see rulings): the eye at AC +5; wing-joints at AC +2; the door at AC +2; blindsight 60 ft; darkvision 120 ft; Rope Trick hides sight and sound both ways.

## SAVE 6 — Days 74–75
Night of Day 74: Dvalin agreed to make a fireproof wrap for the keg, and asked for a shaving of the heart-ember to temper the three suits; Thalorien gave it freely. Gorrim reported a king's forester at the gate-town asking for "Mithrandel". Thalorien went down disguised and watched him: Will Tanner, a young forester waiting for the King's Huntsman, Corvane Ashe, who carries a writ for the elf to answer before the King's Bench and wants his sworn word. Tanner's letter to Ashe warned of the Baron's man Reeve, who said "if the dwarves are stubborn we may have to start it ourselves". The letter was left to go south. Gorrim put the gate on watch: no dwarf looses first.
Riveria kept Forcecage as a scroll and copied Arcane Eye through the night. Thalorien fitted twenty-one red-seam arrows beside her.
Day 75: a parcel for Bell's name-day came with Dagny's wagons: the wooden sword his grandfather made him, lost when the farm burned, and a jar of clover honey. It was paid for in Aranthal by an old man who laughed, and there was thunder from a clear sky when he walked away. Thalorien promised Bell they would look for him together. Thalorien and Riveria read the song-book through: a line scraped out after "who knocks" ("…and the door that stayed—"), a children's rhyme of the Forge and the Flame ("who breaks the round, the cold shall keep"), and a cradle-song they agreed not to read near Ais.
Riveria's Teleport brought the four of them to the White Tree. Aelir knew Lindórë's hand and his own postscript. Calenwë took Thalorien aside and told him that three hundred winters ago a Redseam runner, Skafi, came to Taur-Galen's door with the Fire's brand when the Deep Bell rang, and her father the Keeper had the door barred. She held the bar. She cut the line from the book herself. She asked Thalorien to say nothing to Grorin until she has found the words.
