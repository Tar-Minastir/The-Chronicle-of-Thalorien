# RULINGS & HOUSE RULES

Every rules decision made in this campaign, so it stays consistent across chats. The DM adds an entry whenever a ruling is made on an ambiguous rule, a creative action, or a house rule.

- journal.md is the player-facing BG3-style journal: quests (active/completed/failed, objectives, known facts, leads, dated log), people (attitude Hostile/Wary/Neutral/Friendly/Trusted/Devoted with reasons for each change, summaries of important conversations, promises), Bell's arcs and words learned, promises and debts, lore, places, session entries. ## PLAYER PREFERENCES ON PACING
- When an NPC says something to Thalorien, stop there and let the player answer before moving the scene on. NPCs speak one line and wait, until the topic changes.
- Resolve ONE beat at a time. When danger appears (wings overhead, a waking beast), stop and let the player look, roll checks, or act before resolving it.
- If a roll fails while Thalorien holds Heroic Inspiration, offer the reroll BEFORE narrating the outcome.
- When the player asks "any checks I can roll?", list the fitting checks with bonuses; when asked to roll, roll them all and report each.
- By default, show NPC and companion reactions (Riveria, Bell, Ais, Raul, and anyone else present) to Thalorien's actions and to every notable moment, as fully as fits the scene, in each one's own manner: during and after confrontations, reveals, risks, jokes, quiet moments. The player wants NPCs to feel real. (Player request, Session 2.)
- CAMPAIGN RULE (player request, Session 2): on journeys the party makes camp each night, and every night camp is a played scene, by a fire or a cold camp: companions bond, talk, tell stories, ask questions, train, or study (lore, languages, spellbooks, the hilt, the map). NPCs bring up their own concerns and curiosities. Not skipped by montage unless the player asks. **Never end a night without asking the player first.**
- **NPCs speak only in the world's terms** — never "turn," "slot," "AC," or spell names as rulebook terms; mechanics stay in (( )). (Player request, Session 2.)
- A player message of "…" means Thalorien stays silent and lets the scene (or the NPC addressed) continue.
- Thalorien does not drink alcohol, and he and Riveria keep physical affection private (not in front of others; darkness counts as private).

## CAMPAIGN SETTINGS
- Rules edition: 5e 2024

## TONE & WORLD PREFERENCES (player request)
- Romance: possible, earned through play, never automatic; NPCs can decline. Adults only. Kisses and light, tasteful sensuality on screen; beyond that, fade to darkness.
- Traditional world (content limit): no transgender characters and no LGBTQ+ romances or relationships anywhere in the world. Romance and couples in the setting are between men and women.
- Religion (content limit): the main plot must NOT be built around religion, the faith, or the church turning out to be the true evil "all along." Individual corrupt or misguided people can exist.

## WORLD RULINGS
- Elves live about 1,000 years. Thalorien is about 540, Riveria about 500.
- Dragons follow the Silmarillion model: few, named, legendary; Dragon-Spell vs Wisdom; each has a hidden weakness; dragon-gold curse; mechanics from 5e ancient dragons, adapted; the greatest take mortal form. A dragon knows every piece of its hoard.
- Morgalath the Black is modeled on Ancalagon: a mythic foe of CR 30+; open battle at level 18–19 is certain death. His weakness: the never-closed left eye-socket.
- Law of Elsmere: armed housebreaking on the Hill by night is a hanging matter; trespass alone is a small fine.
- Elendrath's wards bar teleportation into the realm (not out of it, not Sending), except Riveria of the royal blood bringing her company home.
- Moon-sister wells carry speech to and from the Moon-Well; a whisper spoken well away from the rim does not carry.
- The silences are woven of oaths and faith kept; answering an old wrong tied to a silence can mend or still it.
- Morgalath's silver (mirrors, drake collars) is his window: he sees through it when he chooses and it is uncovered; Nondetection hides a person from it; covering it or the Bag of Holding blinds it.

### SAVE 4 rulings (Days 28-48)

- Nature's Veil uses = Wisdom modifier (4). Invisible until end of your next turn; recast to keep it unbroken.
- Dread Ambusher (2024): +10 ft speed on first turn, no extra attack. Dreadful Strike: once per turn, 2d8 psychic (Stalker's Flurry).
- Sharpshooter (2024): no long-range Disadvantage, ignores half/three-quarters cover, no Disadvantage shooting within 5 ft.
- Unseen attackers at long range in darkness: targets learn the line of fire, not the distance; they must search (Perception vs Stealth).
- Pass without Trace defeats scent-tracking of your movement, not detection of your presence close by.
- Commune with Nature does not sense dragons (drakes included). (Re-affirmed; a slip on Day 41 is noted.)
- Cordon of Arrows (2024 wording): fires when a creature first enters within 30 ft on a turn or ends its turn there; 1d6, Dex DC 18 half; a spoken spell near a dozing creature: DC 15 to notice (wind and distance raise it).
- Sleeping creatures are Unconscious; waking from noise: Perception with Disadvantage, DC 15 (DC 20-25 with distance/wind).
- Called shots: wing-joint AC+2 (1/10 max HP to one wing halves flight; 1/5 breaks it; falling takes 1d6 per 10 ft, lands Prone). Mouth AC+5 (a hit spoils a ready breath). Neck/throat: no special effect.

- Bag of Holding: closed, nothing inside can be smelled, heard or sensed; overfilling (>500 lb) tears it. Teleport carries what travellers carry.

- Crafting: legendary items need the master's own hand and time (Dvalin can make two legendary suits by midwinter, or all by the thaw); heart-scales are few. Haldrik's smith's check +13, +2 with the Song of the Anvil, Advantage with Dvalin; an oath at the Anvil lowers the DC.
- Ring: a ring given before witnesses means betrothal among elves. Elves wear it on the right hand (staff/cast hand).
- Group checks (2024): the group succeeds if at least half succeed. The listening-glass: a group song DC 15.
- Enhance Ability gives Advantage, which doesn't stack with Advantage from Help.
- Revivify only within 1 minute; Raise Dead within 10 days (cleric, 500 gp diamond).
- Shops: a fifth off at a merchant's price; haggling is Persuasion (DC 15 for a quarter off with bulk and fame).
- Loot is now more frequent and varied (coin, consumables, scrolls, curios, occasional curses).

## PLAYER PREFERENCE ON STAKES AND BALANCE (Day 74)
- Tension and stakes stay high. This is the most difficult kind of campaign: difficult fights and difficult choices are expected, and plot twists and complications that challenge the player are welcome.
- Balance: the player's decisions decide what happens. The smartest and most creative choices earn the best outcomes, up to preventing a cost entirely or beating a dilemma with a third way.
- Careless play pays in full. Warnings come first.
- Stings and serious plot twists with great danger are allowed, including sudden ones. They must be manageable by the player: there is always something he can do about them, before, during or after. Never a loss that no decision could have changed.
- Dice stay honest.
