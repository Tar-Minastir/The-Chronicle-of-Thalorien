## ESTABLISHED WORLD FACTS
(Never contradict these.)
- Elves live about 1,000 years.
- Uchkeken: hidden elven wood; symbol the White Tree; fell ~380 years ago. Thalorien is the last survivor; he declared it publicly at King Larendil's court.
- The seven silences bind the Weaver (Nûrvael). Signs over Larendil's inner door, in order from the top: the Tree whose roots are its crown (first, highest); a singing bird; a ring of rippling water; a flame in green stone; a crescent moon in a well (Elendrath); an anvil with a star struck from it; a seventh worn smooth. Four have fallen (Uchkeken, Ennor-Lûth, the Silver Mere of Calanor, Taur-Galen). The sixth is the Deep Anvil of Dun Ommar.
- **The seventh silence is Aria, Ais's mother**, a powerful spirit of the high air, woven through the **Whispering Ice** north of Kharûn and singing without pause. Beneath the glacier lies a hollow cavern and a black lake that never freezes; under the lake sleeps **something not of this world** that makes the land fall silent ("what the fire-worm serves"; its heart). Aria: "If I open now… it wakes." "Only her blood can" unmake her. "Not yet." "When the broken ones are whole again." The fifth pair of drakes guards the ice. Morgalath waits for Ais to come and free her.
- Aria's counsel for mending: "Woven of oaths, of faith kept. Broken by wrongs left unanswered. Answer them, and they knit." "You carry the first. Against your heart. It is not dead. Plant it where it fell." "The anvil totters. Its keeper's grief is a crack in it. Mend him, and it holds." "The others: what was sung, sing again. What was put out, kindle." The dragon's weakness: "Eye." Where: "Anvil."
- Silences are woven of oaths and faith kept; unanswered wrongs weaken them, and answering them mends them.
- Morgalath the Black is the One-Eyed Black (see Session 1). His lair is the **Hall of Kings** under Kharûn's peak, on Durvak's gold.
- **Ashwings:** lesser drakes born or warped in the great wyrm's fire; cart-horse size; darkvision; hunt by scent; spit a short cone of burning cinders (the throat glows orange first); thin scales at the wing-joints; heavy for their wings and slow to rise from the ground; roost by day, hunt by night in pairs. They wear **collars of Morgalath's silver**: Enchantment (a leash) twined with a shuttered Divination (a window he can choose to open). A drake's death tugs the leash; he would feel it and roughly where, not who. He looks through the wardens' collars about midnight.
- **Khazad-Ûrbal** was the hold of the Redseam dwarves, kin of the Ironbrows of Dun Ommar ("Redseam and Ironbrow, kin of one Anvil. What one house begins, the other shall finish"). The dragon took it about 300 years ago. Its true name is cut in the threshold-stone of the Smelters' Door.
- **The Smelters' Door** (west flank) opens to a strike on its ore-seam and the true name spoken while the note sounds. Door-hammers: upright = open, we are within; laid flat = closed, gone or dead.
- **The Remembering Fire** in the Great Forge keeps the hold's deeds and oaths; it answers a question asked after a strike on its black anvil and one's name; it gives back what is sworn to it. Water would drown its memory ("bear no water past the third door").
- **The ward of the Deepwater:** a line of frost-metal runes. Nothing born of flame and nothing that serves the Wyrm may pass; fire-born things are quenched beyond it. It still holds.
- **The Long Sleep:** seventeen dwarves (the last of the hold) lie in mail and bronze masks; a listening ward wakes them at sound. They rose when their king came home, and now keep the King's Stair.
- **Durvak's Answer** was forged of Red Seam ore and star-iron sent from the Deep Anvil; it can only be tempered at the Deep Anvil.
- Aldren Wallen's sword: its hilt and shards were kept by Morgalath as a trophy beside his bed; his drakes fear it.
- The two speaking-mirrors were made from silver Morgalath gave Aerthion; he can see through them when uncovered.
- The Book of Silent Mercies is being copied for every healer in the Silverwood by midwinter and in Elsmere by spring. Riveria knows its song for fire-rot.
- Elendrath's wards bar teleportation into the realm from outside, except for the royal blood (Riveria) bringing her company home.
- **The first silence woke** (night of Day 15–16): Thalorien planted the seed hidden in his mother's pendant among the White Tree's roots and sang the whole Song of the White Tree with his mother's memory (the Tree's memory of her). A young White Tree twined from the seed and the old sapling. The light ran across Uchkeken; the Thornwall blossomed white along three leagues. *Fallen three, holding four.* A silence is a lock, not a shield; the dark knows where it is.
- The young tree is not a creature (Revivify can't touch it); it can't be moved; it is rooted to where the song sleeps. It grows with living song (a recording does not move it) and with healthy land. It can turn the hidden ways (wanderers walk out again). Through its deep roots it feels the other silences: the Moon-Well (still, sleeps well, west), the Deep Anvil (rings like hammers, strong but tired), Aria (sings back), and three dark: **Taur-Galen, the Green Flame** (cold, a fire gone out — north-east, the high fells beyond the Ironway), **Ennor-Lûth, the Singing Glade** (holding its breath, waiting for a song — south, beyond the long hills, ~12 days' ride), **the Silver Mere of Calanor** (muddy; "something is sitting in it" — south-west, the low fens where rivers meet the sea; the farthest).
- **The Weaver's loom reaches through broken silences.** At Uchkeken it raised the buried dead as **the woven dead** and a **Loom's Hand** (a giant of dead wood and bone on a cord of grey threads); its cord re-knit from the threads unless the sapling's light lay on it. Destroyed by Thalorien (Day 15). The woven dead were laid back in their cairns (Days 16–19); the Tree welcomed them (a silver leaf at each cairn).
- **The Bough-House** (Sael-ennor's house; a round house of white stone 1.5 miles south-east of the heart; untouched by fire): its silver door opens to a bough of the White Tree brought to it. Within, the stone table and the walls cut with a thousand years of songs, including **the Song of the Bough's Becoming** (four voices or more, sunset to sunrise). The **Bough-Giving** was Uchkeken's feast when the Tree let fall a bough.
- **Morgalath (from Aerthion's pages):** ~1,400 years; the Weaver's hound, who hopes to be "first among its threads" when the loom is finished. **Blind on the left** since Aldren; a drake always flies at his left wing. The **ember** (charm gaze): must see the face, within a hundred paces; never refused. A roar that breaks spears. Casts as a mage (swallows spells, breaks wards, takes a man's shape); never travels except on wings. On the mountain he refused four bindings; fewer away from Kharûn. Breathes from above into the thickest knot; must wait between breaths; will never breathe on Ais. Hates cold (won't set foot on the Whispering Ice) and water. Talks always; calls everyone "little"; counts his gold at dawn; flies the bounds of his lands at dusk. Left the hall when Aerthion hummed the Song of the White Tree.
- **How Aldren died:** the Twin Companies came up Kharûn at dusk with Aria singing a storm; Morgalath burned the Iron Queen's company, then the Thunder Crown's; their captain laughed and the lightning took him — no body, no crown (it vexes Morgalath). Aldren rode Aria's wind onto the dragon's head and drove his sword into the left eye; the blade broke. Morgalath crushed him slowly before Aria. He tells it that Aldren screamed and begged; in truth Aldren shouted Ais's name until the end. Aria came down on him; he drove her into the ice.
- **Binding-steel** (Calimbari): Calimbar's last smiths took what remained to the **Silver Mere of Calanor**, thinking water would keep it safe.
- Dwarven runes and Elendrath: Riveria can Teleport on target to Haldrik's hall (the Ironbrow writ) and to the heart of Uchkeken (the leaf), until about Day 200.
- Queen Aelindra's chamber in Ithilmar (sealed since her death ~500 years ago) is open again.
- **Morgalath the Black was slain in the Cinder Gorge (Day 27).** His body lies in the broken flood-trench under the Ironway's Throat. Scales: hold his fire; dwarven masters could make fire-proof armour (a season's work). The heart: still faintly warm, with a fading grey thread of the Weaver's leash running north — not to be touched. The last sliver of Aldren's blade was drawn from the socket.
- **The Cinder Gorge:** narrow gorge on the Ironway three leagues north of the Toll-stone; the Throat (80 ft wide); the ford and the Black Pool; the old dwarf tower on its spur with a stair and a tunnel to the east rim; the east cave watch-post with arrow-slits and a stair to the ledge; the west ledge and the crack; the saddle a mile and a half north (the only way in from the sky); the dwarven sluice-wall and the tarn half a mile north (cut and wedged, unbroken); the flood-culvert under the road (collapsed). The night wind flows down it from the north; it echoes.
- **"Seven Silences Keep the Loom"**, the dwarven chant (sung by old Sigga Coalbrow): see songs. "When the Flame is out… Kindle it with what the forge has kept." Sigga: the Green Flame of Taur-Galen was first lit from the Redseam forge — the **Remembering Fire** of Khazad-Ûrbal may rekindle it.
- **Songs of the Cinder Gorge spread across the realm** (see songs): above all the dwarven feast-song **"The Company of the Upright Hammer"**, roared in dwarf-halls and dwarven taverns from Dun Ommar to the Harrow and the river wards of Aranthal; also "The Wind's Daughter", "The Boy Who Pulled the Chain", "The Grey Archer of the Cinder Gorge", "Nine Hells and the Long Winter", "The Cinder Gorge". The DM uses them in play: a dwarf in a common room may strike up "Hoom for the hammer!" when the company walks in.
- Hold Monster on a drake paralyzes without killing (no jolt through the collar). With Morgalath dead, the collars, mirrors and coins are dark.

Plan: a small party north to Kharûn (Thalorien, Riveria, Bell, Ais; Raul as scout), the Mask east to Dun Ommar. Thalorien swore to Riveria to live for her; will be the bait alone, seen, without Mind Blank. Talked with Ais on the wall: calm hate; she asked him to stop her if she loses herself (promised).
- **The road:** provisioning at Harrowmere (Tobin Reeve's potion; lean loads; return by Teleport); the Ironway; the burned dwarf-tower: Hrefna Coalbrow's scouts; Olgar healed (Lesser Restoration and the fire-rot song); Kalli sent east with warning; the Smelters' Door verse; Hrefna's question (Thalorien will stand before the thane) and the "two orders." Ais's horse lent; Shadowfax to Raul; Thalorien rides double with Riveria. The pass; the Ashen Waste; night drakes beaten by Pass without Trace; the silver collars. Commune with Nature found a hidden spring and a gully road west.
- **The ice:** Thalorien scouted the patrols (nothing flies west), read the collars with Detect Magic, and learned from Riveria how they work. At the Whispering Ice, Commune with Nature revealed the hollow, the black lake, Aria woven through the ice as the seal, and the unnamed thing beneath. Ais reached for her mother ("not yet"). Thalorien spoke with Aria at the glacier's edge: the seventh silence; only Ais's blood; restore the broken ones; the seed against his heart; the anvil's keeper; "Eye. Anvil." Ais told everything at the slag-heap; she held to "cold" and chose to take from the dragon's house rather than walk into his trap.
- **Khazad-Ûrbal:** Silence on the sleeping sentry; the Smelters' Door opened with the threshold-name; through the fire-trap; the Great Forge and its Remembering Fire; the Answer shown in the flames; the tongs failed twice; drakes came down the shaft; retreat. The Deepwater refuge behind the ward; the wall-map; Hrodna's slates. Thalorien scouted the Hall of Kings by night from an ore-chute (wardens' habits; the midnight eye; Aldren's hilt and shards; Durvak's crown; the covered mirror; the King's Stair door). At dawn, a long rest. Then the oath at the anvil under Silence, and the Fire gave up **Durvak's Answer**. Durvak's bones and hammer carried on Riveria's floating disk to the Long Sleep; through the King's Stair under Fly and Nondetection, the hilt, the shards, and the crown taken from beside the dragon's bed; the hilt given to Ais; the crown set on Durvak's brow; the dead rose and spoke, and keep the stair.

- **Teleport home and Harrowmere** (Days 8–9): a double mishap; horses Revivified; Fenn arrested; banter and potato cakes; Speak with Animals with Shadowfax; horses bought (the dun; the piebald freed). The campaign rule of nightly camp scenes adopted.
- **The road east** (river-folk: Ressa, Tamsin, Dapple/Willow, Corran, Hob, Wynn the deserter, given 10 gp): Uchkeken words taught; the Song of the White Tree written down and given to Riveria; Raul's fear; **Finn's secret** confided to Thalorien alone; the Grey Lanterns' Toll-stone bypassed by night under Pass without Trace and Disguise Self.
- **Dun Ommar:** the barricade; Gorrim; **Haldrik's confession** of the second order and his breakdown; the Khuldrak chorus; the Anvil steadied (the sixth silence holds). Shops (Brisi, Dvalin, Sigrún's bargain, Nim); Morgalath's eye-coins found in the gate-town ("Master Embersson's" purse); Brokk's coin sealed; Brokki's caravan gone west. Durvak's Answer, the shards and Aldren's hilt left with Haldrik.
- **West and back:** Raul sent to Gareth Landrock; Teleport to Greyfold: Saelith, Ithil, Mithrin; the Silverwood camp; Thalorien told Riveria of the Tree.
- **The Thornwall and the heart** (Days 13–16): Ithil sleepwalking under a Weaver-mark — a curse, lifted by Greater Restoration; the woven dead; the Eastern Watch-hall and the opened cairns; the singing on the knuckle-stone held the dead; Thalorien rode the line of light on Shadowfax and destroyed the **Loom's Hand**. His mother's memory in the roots; the seed planted; **the first silence woke**; the Thornwall blossomed. Farewell to his mother ("I am not gone. I am only home"). The companions arrived at dawn and saw him weep; Riveria's curtsy to the Tree, which laughed at "least troublesome." Ais's mother's wind came: "less alone." The killing plan born (Thalorien takes the eye; Ais finishes what her father started). The silver bough fell from the old Tree.
- **The dead brought home** (Days 16–19): cairns rebuilt; Galdhrin's stone; the last dead from the Thornwall; Thalorien spoke for them; the Tree welcomed them. Ithil asked to keep the Tree.
- **The Bough-House** (Day 16–17): opened with the bough; the Song of the Bough's Becoming learned in a day; sung through a night by Thalorien, Riveria, Ais, Finn; shaped by Ithil and Bell (Bell took the knife in the second watch); two shafts fletched by the Tree's own leaves. **Milestone: Ranger 20.**
- **Tending the land** (Days 17–19): Speak with Plants talks with the young tree; the shaping-song remembered; five Plant Growths; drakes walked the Thornwall and could not cross (Finn's report); Sendings to Bell, Raul (no raid; watch the Lanterns), Haldrik (the Answer tempered), King Larendil (rangers). Ais and Riveria's private talk; Bell's "loud hello."
- **The betrothal beneath the White Tree** (night of Day 19): the pendant and braid; Aelindra's ring and braid; vows; bound by Ithil with a living root until dawn; witnessed by Finn, Bell, Ais (and Mother); Finn's halfling wedding song; Ais danced with Bell.
- **Dun Ommar again** (Day 20): Teleport on target via the writ; the Deep Anvil; Ais's choice and blow; the Shard-Arrow; the Answer forged on Aldren's hilt to Thalorien's new song, *Oth-Taur*; the council at Haldrik's table and the plan; Bell asked how to know a heart; Finn's critique (grab, not gaze; who stops a charmed archer) and the doors out; Riveria's choice of shields.

On the road: the Taur-Galen survivors (Calenwë, Aelir, Faradhir) talked down from killing Aerthion and joined; Greyfold gave Ithil leave to ward the Tree; the Guest-Ring oaths; the Thornwall; the White Hart awake.
